FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

Are you a licensed plumbing company?

Fully licensed and insured, working under plumbing permits issued through the York Region inspection system whenever a job calls for one, so Richmond Hill homeowners get paperwork their insurer and future buyer will accept.

Do you take emergency calls in Richmond Hill?

Around the clock for bursts, sewer backups, and total no-water events. Local dispatch means an emergency crew reaches a Richmond Hill address fast at any hour.

Our tank is nineteen years old. Replace it now or wait?

A tank that age is living on borrowed time, and waiting usually means replacing flooring too. We assess it in person and, if it is failing, the truck usually carries the replacement already.

Is poly-B really a problem in a 1994 house?

It becomes one when fittings begin weeping, and Richmond Hill has reached that window. One repair on an accessible line is reasonable, but repeated ceiling stains mean it is time to plan the repipe properly.

What should Oak Ridges well owners test for?

At minimum hardness, iron, and bacteria, ideally each spring. We interpret the results and install treatment sized to them rather than selling every homeowner the same box.

Does the backwater valve cut into my main line?

It does, installed under permit with a proper access box in the basement floor. Richmond Hill homeowners on surcharge-prone streets find it is the single upgrade that most reliably prevents basement sewage backups.

How do your quotes work?

Written, itemized, and provided before work starts, free of charge. Whether the job is a valve swap or a whole-home repipe, the Richmond Hill price you are quoted is the price you pay.

Where besides Richmond Hill do you work?

Oak Ridges, Thornhill, King City, Maple, and Aurora are all regular territory for our crews, in addition to Richmond Hill itself.

Do you serve areas outside of Richmond Hill, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Richmond Hill and nearby communities including Oak Ridges, Thornhill, King City, Aurora, and the surrounding area.
