How this site handles inquiries

Does the same drain slow down or back up repeatedly?

A recurring slow drain or floor-drain backup calls for more than another temporary clearing. Note which fixtures are affected, when the symptoms occur, and whether gurgling or water movement appears elsewhere in the home. If the problem returns, a sewer camera inspection after clearing can help distinguish roots at pipe joints from grease buildup, a defect, or another obstruction. Photograph accessible cleanouts and keep any previous inspection recording or repair notes; that evidence can help define whether the next step is cleaning, monitoring, or evaluating the pipe.

What details help with a Richmond Hill quote?

A power-vented tank and a tankless or high-efficiency unit may use different vent routes and clearances. Photograph the existing vent, nearby finished surfaces, gas connection, and drain access. Ask which changes a proposed conversion requires and how the work can be reached for future service. Those details help distinguish a straightforward replacement from a larger scope involving new venting or drainage. Before selecting a replacement, note the tank capacity, fuel, dimensions, vent type, and the number of people and fixtures drawing hot water. Ask how the proposed model's capacity and recovery rate compare with those details. A tight mechanical area or different connection layout can affect the work, so include photos of access paths and surrounding equipment when requesting an assessment.
